ADBIBE GROWTH CHECKLIST
=======================

Use this checklist to spot funnel leakage before you spend more on traffic.

1. OFFER CLARITY
- Can a new visitor understand what you do in 5 seconds?
- Is the primary CTA obvious above the fold?
- Does the page explain who the service is for and what outcome it creates?

2. TRUST SIGNALS
- Add founder/operator credibility above the fold.
- Show relevant brand names, outcomes, and testimonials.
- Replace generic claims with proof tied to channels, revenue, ROAS, or lead quality.

3. LANDING PAGE UX
- Keep the headline direct and commercially clear.
- Use one primary CTA and one secondary CTA.
- Break long sections into scannable blocks with subheadings and proof points.

4. TRACKING
- Confirm GA4 page_view, form_submit, and primary CTA click events.
- Add clean UTM conventions for every active campaign.
- Check that key conversion events map to the actual business outcome.

5. LEAD FLOW
- Test every form, scheduler, and email action manually.
- Route inbound leads to the right person immediately.
- Define first-response SLAs for warm leads.

6. PAID MEDIA REVIEW
- Match campaign objectives to the actual conversion goal.
- Check whether messaging on ads matches messaging on the landing page.
- Review creative fatigue, budget allocation, search term quality, and audience overlap.

7. CRO PRIORITIES
- Strengthen CTA copy.
- Add objection-handling sections and FAQ blocks.
- Reduce form friction and unnecessary fields.
- Improve mobile readability before adding more traffic.

8. REPORTING
- Track CAC, ROAS, lead quality, pipeline contribution, and conversion rate.
- Stop reporting only clicks and impressions.
- Review results weekly with clear decisions, not just dashboards.
